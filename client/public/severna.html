About Severna

Best school to develop your child’s uniqueness

Severna is a 5-times development award school headquartered in Edinburgh, UK. We believe that every child is unique, and to develop the uniqueness into bright outcomes, they need the best education.
Severna has tons of internationally qualified experts educators, ranging from the role of teacher, consultant and other roles.

With 12 years of experience in both private and home schooling, we have educated more than 15,000 students to successful and happy futures. We open doors to many notorious higher educations.
